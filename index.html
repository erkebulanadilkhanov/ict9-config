5 things to consider when buying a computer

Brain
🧠

CPU (Central Processing Unit)

Executes all operations in the computer. Choose it first — according to your budget, preferences and tasks.

Office: 1800–2200 MHz · 3D graphics / video editing: 2800–3200 MHz
Graphics
🎮

Video card

Builds the image on the monitor. 4 types: Integrated, PCI, AGP, PCI Express. Gaming and video editing need a separate (PCI Express) card.

Temporary
⚡

RAM (Random Access Memory)

Stores currently running programs temporarily. More RAM → faster work. When the computer is turned off, RAM loses its data.

Permanent
💾

HDD or SSD

All files and folders are stored here permanently, even when the computer is off.

Base
🧩

Motherboard (mainboard)

Connects all parts together. Pay attention to its processor interface (socket): the CPU and the motherboard must be compatible.

Intel processors → Socket 1150  |  AMD processors → Socket AM3, FM2, S-AM2
Modern examples: Intel LGA 1700 / LGA 1851, AMD AM5 — the rule is the same: socket must match!

HDD vs SSD

💿 HDD (Hard Disk Drive)⚡ SSD (Solid State Drive)
How it worksSpinning magnetic disks + moving headFlash memory chips, no moving parts
SpeedSlowerMuch faster (system boots in seconds)
Price per GBCheaper — more space for less moneyMore expensive
Shock / noiseCan break if dropped, makes noiseShock-resistant, silent
Best forArchive, big storage of photos/filmsOperating system, programs, games
